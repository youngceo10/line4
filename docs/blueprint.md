# **App Name**: Legislative AI

## Core Features:

- Legislation Monitoring: AI agents monitor and research legislative threats.
- Policy Exposure Mapping: Automatically map policy exposure based on public data and user uploads.
- AI-Driven Threat Research: Research potential legislative threats using AI agents.
- Briefing Generation: Generate briefs on legislative threats identified by the AI. The tool can include relevant financials, SEC filings, and press releases.
- Dashboard Interface: Display a dashboard showcasing potential regulatory risks and AI findings.
- Document Upload: Allow users to upload proprietary documents to enhance the AI's understanding.
- Book a Demo Button: Book a demo functionality

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: McKinsey Blue (#00509E) for a professional and trustworthy feel.
- Secondary color: Light blue (#ADD8E6) to complement the primary blue and provide a sense of calmness.
- Background color: Off-white (#F0F0F0) for a clean and minimalist background, aligning with Swiss design principles.
- Accent color: A deeper shade of blue (#003366) to highlight important elements and CTAs.
- Headline font: 'Helvetica Neue' (or system sans-serif), bold, for a modern and minimalist aesthetic.
- Body font: 'Arial' (or system sans-serif), regular, for clear and concise readability.
- Employ a Swiss-inspired grid layout with precise alignment and ample whitespace.
- Prioritize clarity and readability with a focus on content hierarchy and visual structure.
